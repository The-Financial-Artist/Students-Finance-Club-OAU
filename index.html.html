Welcome to the Foundation Course

This hub is your structured entry point into the four core divisions of finance. Each division is organised into three progressive phases — Foundation, Intermediate, and Advanced — so you can build knowledge systematically, at your own pace.

How to Use This Resource

1

Choose your division from the navigation tabs above — Investment Banking, Global Markets, Global Technology, or Management Consulting.

2

Start at Phase 1 · Foundation regardless of your background. Each concept builds on the previous one.

3

Click any card to open the resource in a new tab. Investopedia articles are great for definitions; YouTube videos are ideal for concept walkthroughs.

4

Progress to Intermediate and Advanced phases once you're comfortable with the foundational concepts. Don't rush — depth matters more than speed.
